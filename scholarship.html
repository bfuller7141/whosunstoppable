Scholarship Info

‍

The The Robles Unstoppable Foundation will be awarding ONE high school senior boy and ONE high school senior girl tournament participant with the I AM UNSTOPPABLE AWARD .

This award comes with a $500 scholarship check for each recipient In order to apply you must meet the requirements below and submit your application items before the deadline on September 15th.

‍

SCHOLARSHIP REQUIREMENTS

  • Must be a registered tournament participant for 2024.
  • Graduating high school student (spring 2024).
  • Academic Achievement-GPA- 3.2 or above.
  • Essay: 500 words or less sharing your Unstoppable story and college goals.
  • Must provide documentation of GPA requirements and acceptance to a 2 or 4-year higher education institution.
