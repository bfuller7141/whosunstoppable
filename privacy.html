Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 04/30/2024

Welcome to the Unstoppable Events, Inc. website (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy outlines how Unstoppable Events, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you access and use our Site.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

‍We gather information that you provide directly to us when you interact with our Site. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you choose to provide. Additionally, we automatically collect certain information regarding your device and your use of our Site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and details about your site interactions.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

‍The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and enhance our Site and services;
  • Respond to your inquiries and offer customer support;
  • Communicate important updates and announcements concerning our services;
  • Personalize your experience on our Site;
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities related to our Site;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities;
  • Fulfill legal obligations;
  • Perform any other purpose we describe at the time of collection.

INFORMATION SHARING

‍We may share your information with third-party service providers who assist us in various operational aspects, including website hosting, data analysis, customer service, and marketing efforts. These providers are permitted to use your personal information solely to deliver their services to us.

We might also disclose your information if legally required, to protect our or others' rights, or in response to valid requests from governmental authorities.

YOUR CHOICES

‍You have the right to opt out of receiving promotional communications from us by following the unsubscribe instructions included in those messages. Please note that we might still send you non-promotional communications, such as those about your account or our ongoing business dealings.

DATA SECURITY

‍We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. However, no transmission method or electronic storage is entirely secure, and we cannot promise the absolute security of your data.

LINKS TO OTHER WEBSITES

‍Our Site may include links to external sites or services not owned or controlled by us. We are not accountable for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Site is designed to support registration and information for an event that includes minors under the age of 18. While the event is open to minors, we do not directly collect personal information from children under 18 through our Site. All personal information required for minors to participate must be provided by their parent or legal guardian. We strongly encourage parents and guardians to actively participate in and monitor their children’s online activities and interests.

CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

‍We may modify this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy regularly to stay informed about how we use and protect your information.

CONTACT US

‍For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at info@whosunstoppable.com.

By using the Unstoppable Events, Inc. website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
