Who's Unstoppable Tournament

This prestigious event brings together top athletes from various states, offering a unique platform to compete at a high level and gain exposure in the wrestling community. With divisions for both boys and girls across High School, Middle School, Elementary, and Open Division the tournament caters to a wide range of age groups and skill levels. Athletes will engage in fierce competition, following structured weight classes and time periods designed to test their prowess and resilience.
‍
Prepare to showcase your skills and determination at the Who's Unstoppable Preseason Wrestling Tournament, the ultimate proving ground for wrestling talent. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a rising star, this tournament is your opportunity to challenge yourself against the best, hone your skills, and earn recognition in front of peers and coaches.

Rules and Eligibility

Wrestlers MUST enter their USA Wrestling Membership number when they register online. Wrestlers that do not have valid USA Wrestling Membership numbers will not be allowed to register. If you have not yet registered for a USA Wrestling Membership, we strongly encourage you to do so at your earliest convenience. For detailed information on registration options, please visit USA Wrestling Membership Registration.

Go To USA Wrestling

Weigh-Ins

Each athlete will be allowed two consecutive attempts to make weight and may not leave the weigh-in area. The second attempt will take place at the LAST CHANCE SCALE in the weigh-in area. All wrestlers must be dressed in a COMPETITION SINGLET for weigh-ins. Reference Divisions for allowance.

All weigh-ins will be at the RAWHIDE Event Center unless otherwise noted.

Uniforms & Gear

Singlets with under briefs or a two-piece uniform is required. Guidelines for the two-piece uniforms are as follows: wrestlers can wear compression or board shorts and a tight-fitting shirt. The shorts shall not be baggy or have pockets, buttons, or snaps.
‍
Girls MUST wear a compression Women’s Singlet or Two-piece uniform.

Shoes with laces are to be secure with a DOUBLE KNOT.

Folkstyle Rules

National Federation of State High School Association scoring rules with USA Wrestling modifications will govern the competition. These USA Wrestling modifications include the following:

  • New TakeDown Rule in effect

  • New One Point Of Contact = INBOUNDS

  • New Technical Fall Criteria

  • New Near-Fall Criteria

  • Headgear is not MANDATORY for all competitors but recommended.

  • Mouth guards are not required for wrestlers (even wrestlers with braces).

  • Rest time in between matches is a minimum of 15 minutes.
