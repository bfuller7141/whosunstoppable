Eligibility & Rules

‍

Athlete Registration

    • All athletes must register online and provide accurate personal information, including full name, age, and appropriate division.
    • Athletes found to be registered outside of their eligible age group will be
      disqualified and removed from the event.
      ‍

Weigh Ins/ Medical

  • Athletes must weigh in wearing an unaltered competition singlet.
  • No weight-cutting is permitted once weigh-ins begin.
  • Missed weight? No problem. Athletes who miss weight will be moved up automatically without penalty.
  • The Chief Medical Officer has the final say on all medical clearances. Their decisions are final and not subject to appeal.

‍

INJURIES & Illness

  • If an athlete becomes injured or ill during the event, they will not be allowed to continue without medical clearance.
  • All decisions regarding injury or illness will be made by the Chief Medical Officer and are final.

‍

Coach Access

To access the competition floor and/or the Coach Hospitality Room, a valid Coach Floor Pass is required.

  • Elite Coach Floor Pass: Includes access to the competition floor and the hospitality room.
  • Standard Coach Floor Pass: Includes competition floor access only.

The Robles Unstoppable Foundation – “I AM UNSTOPPABLE” Scholarship Award

The The Robles Unstoppable Foundation is proud to present the “I AM UNSTOPPABLE” Award to one high school senior boy and one high school senior girl participating in the Who’s Unstoppable tournament.

Each recipient will receive a $500 scholarship check to support their academic journey.

To be considered, all application materials must be submitted by September 15th and meet the following eligibility criteria:

‍

SCHOLARSHIP REQUIREMENTS

  • Must be a registered participant in the 2026 Who’s Unstoppable Tournament
  • Must be a graduating high school senior
  • Minimum GPA of 3.2 (must submit official documentation)
  • Must have proof of acceptance to a 2-year or 4-year accredited college or university
  • Submit a 500-word (or less) essay describing:
    • Your Unstoppable story
    • Your goals for college and beyond

Remote Weigh-in information

Remote Weigh-in Site Criteria

If you would like to be approved as an official Remote Weigh-In Site for the Who’s Unstoppable Preseason Nationals, you must meet the following requirements:

‍

  1. Weigh-In Schedule Compliance:
    • Your weigh-in schedule must align with team and individual travel plans.
    • Athletes must be able to weigh in without delays or disruptions to tournament travel.
    • Remote Weigh-In Date & Time:
      • October 3, 2026
      • 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM (Local Time)
      • No Early Weigh-Ins Permitted at Remote Locations
  2. Adherence to Designated Times:
    • All Remote Weigh-In Sites must follow the approved schedule issued by tournament organizers.
    • Any deviations must be pre-approved by the tournament committee.
    • Sun Devil Fitness Complex ONLY offers early weigh-in:
      • Early Weigh-Ins: 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM
      • Regular Weigh-Ins: 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  3. TrackWrestling Integration:
    • Each site must have a designated statistician with TrackWrestling login credentials.
    • That individual is responsible for entering official weights directly into the TrackWrestling system during the weigh-in window.
    • Weigh-ins must be electronically closed at 6:00 PM local time.

Apply To Become an Approved Weigh-in site

  1. Visit the " Who's Unstoppable Preseason Nationals" website.
  2. Navigate to the "Contact" section.
  3. Select the option for "Remote Weigh-In" from drop down menu & submit your inquiry.
  4. In your message, confirm your agreement to meet all listed criteria.

Email Application Must Include:

  1. Name of Weigh-In Site
  2. Full Site Address
  3. Contact Person’s Name
  4. Contact Phone Number
  5. Contact Email Address
