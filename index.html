Elegibility & Rules

For Athletes

During the online registration process, all wrestlers are REQUIRED to provide their USA Wrestling Membership number. Wrestlers without valid USA Wrestling Membership numbers will be unable to complete their registration. If you have not obtained a USA Wrestling Membership yet, we highly recommend doing so at your earliest convenience. For detailed information on registration options, please visit the USA Wrestling Membership Registration page.
‍
We are thrilled to offer divisions for both boys and girls across Elementary, Middle School, High School, and OPEN divisions.

Go To USA Wrestling

For Coaches

All coaches that wish to receive the discounted admission entry to gain access to the hospitality room and the competition floor to coach athletes must have a current USA Wrestling Leader Membership Card. All USA Wrestling Leader Membership Cards MUST be attained prior to the event, due to mandatory background checking.

PLEASE ALLOW UP TO 30 DAYS FOR COMPLETION OF USA WRESTLING LEADER MEMBERSHIP/BACKGROUND CHECK PROCESS.

If you don't have a USA Wrestling Leader Membership Card you can purchase a FLOOR PASS to gain access to the competition floor, but won't have access to the hospitality room.

Go To USA Wrestling

Floor Pass

Unstoppable Events Inc., a Non-Profit 501(c)(3), deeply values the hard work and dedication of coaches in training their athletes. As a token of our appreciation, we are pleased to offer a discounted entry for coaches.

To avail of the discounted entry, it is imperative to possess a valid 2024-25 USA Coaching Card. This will provide General Admission and Floor Access for coaching your athlete over the course of both days, along with access to the Coaching Hospitality Room. Note: USA Coaching Card Background check at a minimum must be in process and documentation showing PROOF is required at time of entry.

Alternatively, for coaches who do not intend to attend any other FUTURE USA Wrestling SANCTIONED events, the option of purchasing a Floor Pass is available. This pass will afford General Admission and Floor access for coaching your athlete throughout both days, but will not include access to the Coaching Hospitality area.

2024 Tournament

Weigh-Ins

Each athlete will be allowed two consecutive attempts to make weight and may not leave the weigh-in area. The second attempt will take place at the LAST CHANCE SCALE in the weigh-in area. All wrestlers must be dressed in a COMPETITION SINGLET for weigh-ins. Reference Divisions for allowance.

All weigh-ins will be at the RAWHIDE Event Center unless otherwise noted.

Weigh-in information

Uniforms & Gear

Singlets with under briefs or a two-piece uniform is required. Guidelines for the two-piece uniforms are as follows: wrestlers can wear compression or board shorts and a tight-fitting shirt. The shorts shall not be baggy or have pockets, buttons, or snaps.

Girls MUST wear a compression Women’s Singlet or Two-piece uniform.

Shoes with laces are to be secure with a DOUBLE KNOT.

Tournament Rules

Folkstyle Rules

National Federation of State High School Association scoring rules with USA Wrestling modifications will govern the competition. These USA Wrestling modifications include the following:

  • New TakeDown Rule in effect

  • New One Point Of Contact = INBOUNDS

  • New Technical Fall Criteria

  • New Near-Fall Criteria

  • Headgear is not MANDATORY for all competitors but recommended.

  • Mouth guards are not required for wrestlers (even wrestlers with braces).

  • Rest time in between matches is a minimum of 15 minutes.
