Elegibility & Rules

Athlete Eligibility & Divisions

All wrestlers must register online to compete. A valid USA Wrestling Membership is NOT required for participation in this event.

We’re excited to offer divisions for boys and girls across Elementary, Middle School, and High School levels. Divisions are structured by age and grade eligibility to ensure fair and competitive brackets for all athletes.

Be sure to review the finalized weight classes and time periods for your division before registering.

Coach Access & Floor Pass Info

To gain access to the Coach Hospitality Room—where complimentary food and beverages will be provided—you must purchase the Elite Coach Floor Pass.

Two Options for Coaches:

  • Elite Coach Floor Pass – Grants full access to:
    • The competition floor to coach your athletes
    • The Coach Hospitality Room (free food & drinks)
  • Standard Floor Pass – Allows access to:
    • The competition floor only
    • Does not include hospitality room access

Passes must be purchased during registration or at check-in. No exceptions will be made at the door.

Coach Access Policy

Unstoppable Events Inc., a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, proudly recognizes the dedication and commitment of coaches who support and guide their athletes. To show our appreciation, we’re offering two access options for coaches attending the event:

  • Elite Coach Floor Pass
    • Includes General Admission.
    • Full access to the competition floor to coach your athlete(s).
    • Access to the Coach Hospitality Room, where complimentary food and beverages will be available.
  • Standard Floor Pass
    • Includes General Admission.
    • Access to the competition floor to coach your athlete(s).
    • Does not include access to the Coach Hospitality Room.

Both passes are valid for the full duration of the event and can be purchased during registration or at check-in.

The registration cut-off date for this event is October 1.

Weigh-Ins

Each athlete will be allowed two consecutive attempts to make weight and may not leave the weigh-in area. The second attempt will take place at the LAST CHANCE SCALE in the weigh-in area. All wrestlers must be dressed in a COMPETITION SINGLET for weigh-ins. Reference Divisions for allowance.

All weigh-ins will be at the RAWHIDE Event Center unless otherwise noted.

Weigh-in information

Uniforms & Gear

Singlets with under briefs or a two-piece uniform is required. Guidelines for the two-piece uniforms are as follows: wrestlers can wear compression or board shorts and a tight-fitting shirt. The shorts shall not be baggy or have pockets, buttons, or snaps.

Girls MUST wear a compression Women’s Singlet or Two-piece uniform.

Shoes with laces are to be secure with a DOUBLE KNOT.

Tournament Rules

Folkstyle Rules

National Federation of State High School Association scoring rules with USA Wrestling modifications will govern the competition. These USA Wrestling modifications include the following:

  • New TakeDown Rule in effect
  • New One Point of Contact = INBOUNDS
  • New Technical Fall Criteria
  • New Near-Fall Criteria
  • Headgear is NOT mandatory for all competitors but recommended.
  • Mouth guards are not required for wrestlers (even wrestlers with braces).
  • Rest time in between matches is a minimum of 15 minutes.
